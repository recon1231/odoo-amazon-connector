What you get

🛒 Orders → Sale orders → Invoices

Orders are imported every 15 minutes. Each one becomes a confirmed sale order on the right warehouse. FBA deliveries are validated automatically, and the invoice is created for you.

📦 Listings & stock sync

Every SKU is mapped to an Odoo product by internal reference, barcode or alias. Free stock is pushed to Amazon with a safety buffer, and the app warns you before you oversell.

🏭 FBA inventory

See fulfillable, inbound, reserved and unfulfillable stock per SKU, the gap with your Odoo FBA warehouse, and how many days of stock you have left.

💰 Settlements → journal entries

Each payout is split into sales, tax, referral, FBA, storage, ads, promotions, refunds and reimbursements. It is booked as one balanced entry ready for bank reconciliation.

↩️ Returns & claims

A kanban board for returns, from new to waiting, received, claim and closed. Disposition, refund, reimbursement and net loss are tracked for each return.

📊 Profit dashboard

Sales, fees, net margin after cost and fees, best sellers, problem products, payouts, sync health and a "Needs you" to-do bar.

Setup in 5 minutes

  1. Seller Central → Apps & Services → Develop Apps: create a private SP-API app (roles: Orders, Inventory, Finance, Listings, Reports).
  2. Authorize it and copy the LWA client id, client secret and refresh token.
  3. In Odoo: Amazon Hub → Configuration → Seller Accounts → New, paste the credentials and click Test connection.
  4. Pick your warehouses, your generic Amazon customer and your settlement accounts. Done.
